# Who Gets Jensen Huang Next? — story bible

**Logline / tone.** A fast, wry news-explainer (suspense with dark humour): a famous founder is out, everyone wants him, and the money says nobody gets him. Style: **Midnight Blue** (cold navy, sodium-orange practicals). Voice is a narrator; nobody is lip-synced. 16:9, 1080p, SFX only (no music).

## Cast (one signature colour each)
| id | role | outfit | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| JENSEN | the hero | Nvidia-green suit jacket, no hat | tired, determined, sheepish; the only one who changes expression a lot |
| ELON | first suitor | orange tee | smirks, thumbs up |
| MORRIS | TSMC founder | yellow suit + glasses | offers the baton |
| LISA | AMD, distant relative | red dress + bun | awkward dinner |
| MASA | SoftBank | blue suit | stamps SOLD, carries cash |
| BOARD / CROWD / REPORTER / SPY | extras | plain ink, no colour | silhouettes |

## Locations
- **Office** (interiorCool, night window skyline; warm lamp at the end for "my old desk").
- **Rooftop** (night, sodium skyline, wind): Elon scenes.
- **Street** (night, rain, lamp posts): Jensen alone, reporter, spy.
- **Boardroom / hearing room** (cold blue, long table, window).
- **Diner** (warm neon, booth): 1993 start-over.
- **Void / blueprint** (navy grid): titles, odds boards, data cards, diagrams.

## Motifs
- **Green** = Nvidia/Jensen (chips, shares, glow). **Orange** = Elon. Odds cards always in the same navy card style.
- The **old desk** opens and closes the video (callback).
- Every number the narrator says is on screen, synced to the word.

## Act plan
1. Cold open (0–14 s): firing, offer, bets, 1,600 employees. 2. Recap (14–24 s). 3. The post (24–45 s). 4. Polymarket + suitors (45–110 s). 5. The rejoin bet (110–136 s). 6. Why care + two dates (136–152 s). 7. Closing joke (152–165 s).
Sound: SFX only; ambience beds per location (rain, wind, city, hum, murmur, roomtone).
